# Native Bink1 codec on current game main

The portable original-format SJPi Bink1 decoder is compiled and tested here **without replacing the modern Windows movie host**. The independent video, RGB surface conversion, audio packet/FFT, PCM16, sample clocks and original BIK diagnostic utilities live in the reconstruction library, not in a game-specific shell. No proprietary game assets or original DLL are embedded in this change.

Real-file validation against the connected original archive now covers 38 SJPi movies (11,266 frames) across global cinematics, walkthroughs, and the five Spud Skate clips. The native video decoder completed every frame, but this is decode-coverage evidence, NOT pixel-parity certification. A temporary four-byte SJPi-to-BIKf signature change is enough for FFmpeg to decode the audio packets, but it does not provide a valid independent video reference: FFmpeg rejects or misdecodes many SJPi video frames after that change. An original-DLL capture or another independently validated SJPi video reference is still needed for pixel parity.

The native audio decoder and PCM16 serializer were tested on all 22 available original audio-bearing tracks against FFmpeg. All sample counts matched and the maximum signed-16-bit sample difference was one LSB. The terminal partial audio packet is handled explicitly; a partial declaration in an intermediate packet is rejected. Original-DLL PCM parity and a real DirectSound device/synchronization test remain outstanding, so native A/V playback stays opt-in. The 23 Fireworks show clips are not present in the connected asset archive and have not received original-file parity testing.
